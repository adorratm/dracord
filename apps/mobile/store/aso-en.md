## Long description
Dracord is an ad-free community client with Turkish-first UI: guilds, channels, DMs, friends, notifications; rich chat (threads, pins, polls, reactions). LiveKit voice rooms; game channels and watch party; guild admin (roles, invites, moderation). Dark Dracula theme.

Mobile screen sharing is limited by the OS; full screen share is on the desktop client.

Core experience stays free and without ads; optional premium may come later.
