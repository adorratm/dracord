## Long description
Dracord is a full native community client (no WebView) with guilds, DMs, friends, search, and notifications; rich chat (threads, pins, polls, reactions). LiveKit voice/video; game channels and watch party; guild admin (channels, roles, invites, moderation). Dark Dracula theme. Turkish-first UI.

Mobile screen sharing is limited by the OS; full screen share is available on the desktop client.
